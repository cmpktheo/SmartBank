Cards :5104 Postgres Redis

Card lifecycle + PAN protection. Self-contained — no gRPC, no events yet.

Functionality

What it owns

  • GET /api/cards, GET /api/cards/{id}, POST …/freeze | …/unfreeze (both need Idempotency-Key), PATCH …/limits, POST …/reveal-cvv.
  • CVV reveal rate-limited in Redis (sb:crd:reveal:{userId}, 5 per 10 min → 429 CARD_REVEAL_LIMIT).
  • PAN encrypted via EnvPanProtector (CARD_HMAC_KEY, CARD_AES_KEY); MediatR FreezeCardCommand.
