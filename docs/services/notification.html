Notification :5105 Postgres RabbitMQ no Redis · no JWT

Async-only consumer — writes a notification log from transfer events.

Functionality

What it owns

  • MoneyTransferredConsumer (raw RabbitMQ.Client) → notification_log {eventId UNIQUE, channel, template, recipient, subject, body, payload}.
  • GET /api/notifications/log?take (max 100; Operations role outside Dev). No login/JWT middleware — not user-facing.
  • Auto-migrates in Dev; dedupes on eventId so redeliveries are safe.
